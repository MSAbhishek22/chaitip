    How it works

    1. Pick an amount. ₹5, ₹10, ₹20 — priced live in BCH.
    2. Connect your wallet. Paytaca or Selene, via WizardConnect. Nothing to install here.
    3. Approve one payment. The tip goes straight to the vendor's address. A ticket receipt proves it on-chain.
